◆ is_negative_succ()

template<typename Node, typename Mapping, typename GetWeight, typename PointTo>
auto digraph_detail::is_negative_succ ( const Node & handle,
const Mapping & dist,
GetWeight && get_weight,
const PointTo & point_to )->bool

Predecessor negativity check mirrored for successor relaxation.

The successor policy keeps \(d_u \ge d_v - w(u,v)\) for point_to[u] == (v, edge), so a negative cycle violates the inequality in the opposite direction: \(d_u < d_v - w(u,v)\).
